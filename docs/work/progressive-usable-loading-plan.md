## Resolved architecture

### C1 — Source-owned composition reuse

Current `contract-compose.ts` wraps provenance outside artifact registration and reallocates redline output; retained wrappers do not retain underlying registered run/block dependencies. Store completed derived provenance/redline groups in the existing source-owned artifact store with explicit dependencies. Cache keys include ordered resolved inputs and relevant lineage, aliases, view, numbering/reference semantics. Retain displayed/current/in-flight roots and their dependencies independently of the bounded optional LRU. Do not increase the 256 optional budget or introduce a second unbounded cache. Preserve cancellation, generator yielding, correct invalidation and disposal. Unchanged materialized inputs preserve resolved/prepared/page identity.

### C2 — Buffered DOM, scheduler admission and acknowledgement

Replace the renderer's first-single-page pause with initial-batch state: exact closed pages, pagination complete and full snapshot complete are separate facts. In an active visible document, continue through pages one/two, mount one page per bounded scheduled append unit behind the quiet frame, flush them, then reveal the three-page or completed-short batch atomically. The viewer acknowledges only that generation/epoch's displayable batch after a real visible opportunity. An acknowledgement is never required to obtain the third page or short-document pagination completion.

Separate **hidden initial DOM in a visible tab**, **parked/background preparation**, **a hidden browser tab**, and **missing host/profiler**. Parked admitted work proceeds without a physical visible-paint acknowledgement. Keep existing scheduler background/alternative suspension while `document.hidden`, foreground pacing and resource admission: hidden-tab work may suspend; work without host/profiler may wait. Visibility, priority, activation, cancellation and host changes must resume or cancel correctly without orphaned waiters. Do not force background dispatch or fabricate paint to guarantee completion. An outstanding visible acknowledgement must be cancelable/reclassifiable when its owner becomes parked/inactive; stale generations/epochs cannot release a newer job. Preserve foreground priority and current-page geometry ownership.

Before-threshold failure has local recoverable progress/error. After-threshold failure preserves actual revealed pages with tail retry. Empty pagination retains the current explicit no-pages failure semantics, never hangs waiting for three. Warm/current/retained activation uses its existing complete pages immediately. Full custom metadata is built once after pagination; hidden initial mounts do no highlight discovery/geometry scans, and complete-only controllers/projection initialize once after the complete snapshot/DOM is ready. Completion cannot steal help/input focus.

Keep the single saved HelpSession/modal in a lightweight persistent route admission owner, above SavedContract module, host, readable data, pagination and search.
